# Privacy Policy

PingDot is an open-source app by Schuon Technologies GmbH (Schub), provided free
of charge and as is.

## No data collection

PingDot does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account,
no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting. Settings are stored only on
your Mac, and so is the connection history (latency and outages of the last 24
hours, for the *Connection history* window). It never leaves your Mac.

## Network connections

To do its job, PingDot contacts:

- the ping targets you configure (by default `1.1.1.1` from Cloudflare and
  `8.8.8.8` from Google), via ICMP or a TCP connection on port 443;
- your router, to check whether it is reachable;
- `www.apple.com`, to check that DNS and HTTPS connections work;
- only in the version from GitHub or Homebrew: `api.github.com`, once a day, to
  see whether a newer version exists. You can turn this off under
  *Settings → Check for updates*. The App Store version does not do this.

These are plain connectivity and version checks. No data about you or your Mac is sent — but
like any network connection, the operators of these targets can see your IP
address. You can change the targets under *Settings → Targets*.

## Downloads

If you download PingDot from the Mac App Store, GitHub or Homebrew, their
privacy policies apply to the download itself.
