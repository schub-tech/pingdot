## Description

PingDot puts a dot in your menu bar that shows whether your internet works. One glance answers the question every frozen video call raises: is it the app, or is it my connection?

• Green: the last 10 pings came back.
• Yellow: packets are getting lost, or pings get through but websites won't load.
• Red: the internet is down, or there is no network at all.

PingDot pings Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) and Google (8.8.8.8) in parallel and only turns red when both stop answering. One provider having a bad day is not your outage.

WHERE IS IT BROKEN?
Click the dot to see the last pings per target with average, minimum, maximum and loss. Below, PingDot checks your network interface, your router, DNS and a real HTTPS connection, and names the likely cause in plain words, such as "Wi‑Fi is off" or "Router reachable, internet not". Copy diagnostics puts a text report on your clipboard for the support chat with your provider.

WHEN IT HELPS
• Video calls that freeze
• Train, hotel and café Wi‑Fi, including login pages
• Phone hotspots that drop in tunnels
• Home office: restart the router or call the provider?
• Office networks that block ping: PingDot notices that the web still works and suggests switching to TCP

SETTINGS
• Add your own targets, such as your NAS, router or company proxy
• Check every 0.5, 1, 2 or 5 seconds
• ICMP ping or TCP connect
• Latency in ms next to the dot
• Symbols instead of colours for colour-blind users
• Launch at login

PRIVACY
No tracking, no analytics, no account. PingDot only contacts the targets you set, your router and apple.com for the DNS and HTTPS checks.

Lightweight: about 0.2 % CPU and 38 MB of memory. Open source under the MIT license.
